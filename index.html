01 / About

The neuropsychobiological basis of aggression.

Within the TRR 379, I try to unravel the neuropsychobiological causes of aggression. Both non-invasive brain stimulation and neuroimaging are central to this work.

View Research Profile ↗

My research investigates how prefrontal inhibitory-control systems contribute to reactive and proactive aggression, and whether non-invasive brain stimulation can modulate these responses. I combine these approaches with behavioral paradigms and functional MRI.

Recent Awards: 2026 Lagerspetz Award, International Society for Research on Aggression · 2025 Aachen Prize for Gender-Specific Research in Medicine.
