Methods & technical skills

01

Brain stimulation

Over 1,000 hours with tDCS, tACS, HD-tDCS, single-pulse TMS, and repetitive TMS.

02

MRI acquisition & multimodal integration

Over 400 hours of MRI measurements on a Siemens MAGNETOM Prisma Fit 3T; over 300 hours integrating tES with resting-state and task-based fMRI, including naturalistic viewing.

03

Structural & functional imaging analysis

Proficient with SPM and FSL; CONN for functional connectivity and CAT12 for structural analysis. Experience also includes EEG analysis with BrainVision Analyzer.

Clinical neuropsychology

Over 800 hours assessing psychiatric, neurological, and forensic populations, including neuropsychological assessment and diagnosis.

Data analysis & research tools
